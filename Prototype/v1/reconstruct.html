STAGE 02 — IN DEVELOPMENT

Probable structure reconstruction

For a flagged or partially known site, this stage will generate a probable 3D visualization of the original structure — always shown with a confidence score, never presented as historical fact.

  • Takes satellite imagery, existing photographs, and archaeological drawings as input
  • Produces a visual/3D reconstruction of the probable original form
  • Displays a confidence score alongside every reconstruction
  • Intended output: "AI-assisted probable reconstruction — 72% confidence," not a claim of certainty
